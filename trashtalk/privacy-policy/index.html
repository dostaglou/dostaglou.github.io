TrashTalk

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 13, 2026

TrashTalk is a garbage and recycling schedule app published by foreignorchid.

Information stored on your device

TrashTalk stores the schedules, collection types, custom labels, reminder settings, and related app preferences you enter. The app uses this information to display your collection calendar and prepare reminders. This information is stored in TrashTalk’s app-specific storage on your device.

TrashTalk does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or location. TrashTalk does not send your schedules or reminder information to the developer or to a TrashTalk server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, TrashTalk schedules local reminders through your device’s operating system. The reminder content is generated from your schedules stored on your device; TrashTalk does not send it to a push notification server. You can change notification permissions in your device settings.

Sharing and third parties

The current version of TrashTalk does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. The developer does not collect or share your schedule information with third parties.

If you enable device-level backups, your device or operating system provider may handle app data under its own settings and policies.

Data deletion and retention

Your schedules and preferences remain on your device until you remove them. You can remove individual schedules in the app. To delete TrashTalk’s locally stored app data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall TrashTalk.

TrashTalk does not provide user accounts, and the developer does not keep a server-side copy of your app data.

Security

TrashTalk stores app data in its app-specific storage on your device and does not transmit that data to the developer. Your device’s security settings, such as its screen lock, help protect locally stored information.

Changes to this policy

If TrashTalk’s data practices change, this policy will be updated. The revised policy will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
